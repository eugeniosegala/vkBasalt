# vkBasalt fork asset provenance

`assets/mako-vkbasalt.webp` is original MAKO fork identity artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 15 October 2026 from the maintainer's brief: a blue-and-white MAKO shark splitting light through a prism into three shader-filter panels. The existing MAKO, MAKO Renderer, MAKO Gym, MAKO Traces, and MAKO Shaders artwork was visually inspected to match the neon pixel-art style and square composition; no source images were supplied to the generation tool and no third-party source artwork is intentionally incorporated.

The 1254×1254 PNG original (1,770,710 bytes) was encoded without resizing using `cwebp -q 90 -m 6 -sharp_yuv -metadata none`, producing a 229,012-byte WebP. The result was inspected at full size and at the README's 256-pixel display size. The exact prompt, PNG original, encoding comparison, preview, and generation record are retained locally in the MAKO checkout's ignored `engine/out/vkbasalt-artwork/` directory.
